What it is, in plain terms

Stratolink is a classroom kit that lets students build, launch, and track a small balloon that rises higher than airplanes fly — then drifts for hundreds of miles, sending live altitude, temperature, and position the entire way.

The class names the mission together. Every student appears on it as a Mission Commander. The data is not a simulation.

What your students actually do

  1. Build. Assemble and seal the lightweight payload. Lift comes from a sealed cartridge — no tanks, no open flames.
  2. Launch. Fill the balloon, name the mission, let it go. In minutes it is a dot in the sky.
  3. Track. Watch it climb and drift on the mission dashboard from their desks.
  4. Learn from the data. Graph the climb, calculate rates, follow coordinates across real places.
