02 / ARCHITECTURE · PAYLOAD SYSTEM DIAGRAM

Functional Spacecraft Architecture.

The Stratolink payload runs the same end-to-end TT&C stack as an orbital cubesat: integrated sensor suite, narrow-band RF uplink to a ground gateway, packet forwarding over the public internet to the mission operations server, and a live operator surface. The lift vehicle — a high-altitude balloon — is functionally a launch stage. The payload is the mission.

SPECTRUM / LICENSING

No amateur radio license required.

The payload transmits over LoRaWAN — a long-range RF protocol operating in the unlicensed 902–928 MHz ISM band. The gateway forwards each packet over standard internet to the mission operations server. No HAM exam, no call sign, no instructor certification.

OPERATIONAL EQUIVALENCE

Same cognitive task as a USSF Mission Operator.

Cadets read telemetry, interpret sensor fusion data, recognize anomalies, and apply contingency procedures. The platform compresses the satellite-operations skill stack into a multi-hour observable mission.

05 / STRATEGIC RATIONALE · FOR DECISION MAKERS

The cadet who tracks a satellite mission at thirteen
applies to Space Force at eighteen.

ARG-01

Space Force is the most technical branch.

Operators need fluency in satellite communications, telemetry interpretation, sensor fusion, mission planning, and contingency response — all of it on day one. Stratolink delivers experiential reps in every one of those domains before the recruit ever sees a recruiter.

ARG-02

Hands-on at the right age.

The cognitive and emotional foundation for mission-operations thinking is built between ages 11 and 16. Watching your own satellite cross an ocean is the same emotional pattern as monitoring a real spacecraft in a real ops center. We are scaffolding the operator's intuition early.

ARG-03

No equivalent platform exists.

No other classroom tool teaches end-to-end satellite communications experientially at this scale or price point. Robotics kits don't transmit. Model rockets don't have payloads. Stratolink is the only K-12-accessible platform that runs a real spacecraft mission stack.

07 / CURRICULUM ALIGNMENT · MISSION DOMAIN MAPPING

Mission Domains → Space Force Career Fields.

Every mission domain Stratolink trains maps to a defined Space Force operational specialty.

Satellite Communications Architecture

AFSC · 1C6X1

End-to-end uplink/downlink path, antenna gain, link budget, frequency planning, packet structure. Students set up and validate the comms link before launch.

Telemetry Interpretation & Sensor Fusion

AFSC · 1C5X1

Reading multi-channel telemetry under noise, recognizing anomalies, cross-validating one sensor against another (TF-003 GPS freeze · pressure fallback).

Atmospheric & Orbital Science

AFSC · 15WX

Standard atmosphere, gas laws, buoyancy and equilibrium, jet stream behavior. Foundation for orbital mechanics, re-entry, and high-altitude science.

Mission Planning & Contingency Operations

AFSC · 13S

Pre-flight prediction, in-flight monitoring, contingency procedure execution, post-mission debrief. The standard ops-officer skill stack at K-12 scale.

Data Analysis & Mission Debrief

AFSC · 1N0X1

Reconstruction from archived telemetry, anomaly root-cause, performance against prediction. Builds the analytical fluency intelligence operators rely on.

Spacecraft & Payload Systems

AFSC · 14NX

Sensor suite design, power budgeting, thermal envelope, payload mass, fault-tolerant operation. The same trade space as a real cubesat program — at student scale.
