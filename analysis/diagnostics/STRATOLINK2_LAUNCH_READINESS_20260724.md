v15. The exact image is installed and restored after profiling, its immutable

and regional credential checks. Historical rows below are retained as the
adversarial record and may describe superseded intermediate candidates.

Prepared 2026-07-24/25 during the final connected-board review. This is a
gate sheet, not a declaration of readiness. `PROVEN` means the exact claim has
direct evidence; `PARTIAL` means important layers are proven but an end-to-end

## Evidence matrix

Balance-candidate update: the purpose-built dual `ALD910025SALI` SAB MOSFET
eliminates the TLV8801 divider/op-amp. Its source-bound typical model gives
114.683 µA net equalization at the worst initial 4% mismatch and 9.298 h at
25 °C, with 114.042 mV balanced-cell offset margin. ALD does not limit its
current curve or temperature coefficients, however, and the industrial -40 °C
rating misses Flight 3's coldest telemetry by 2.1 °C. It is therefore a strong
alternative for CAP-XX review and exact-assembly HIL, not a qualified drop-in.

Flight-power evidence correction: 3.322 V is the minimum *reported* Flight-3
value, not a measured brownout threshold. Flown commit `c4bd109` converted the
VSTOR divider with a fixed 3.3 V reference and explicitly ignored runtime VDDA.
Once the buck entered dropout and VDDA tracked VSTOR, the ratiometric conversion
approached a false ~3.32 V plateau and hid the actual rail. Current source now
requires successful ADC initialization and calibration, refreshes VREFINT before
every VSTOR read, and returns zero on any initialization, calibration, conversion,
or reference-validity failure so load authorization fails closed. The conversion
is range-checked in 32-bit space before narrowing; a regression reproduces and
rejects the former 396,000 mV-to-2,784 mV wrap alias;
`flight3_vstor_floor_audit.py` binds both implementations and the five
post-launch plateau rows. The 3.32 V value remains a conservative energy-model
endpoint only. Actual BOR, ADC accuracy, and TX/tier floors require the exact
PPK2 low-rail sweep.

`low_rail_load_audit.py` now bounds why the present 3.6 V GPS and 3.0 V TX
floors are plausible but not qualified. Using TI's pessimistic 2.9 ohm buck
high-side resistance, the modeled 90 milliohm cold-cap ESR, and u-blox's 100 mA
startup surge leaves 3.301 V at the GPS floor: safely above the strapped 2.7 V
GNSS V_IO minimum, but 11 mV below the 3.312 V buck setpoint. The +14 dBm
44 mA screen leaves 2.868 V at the TX floor, above the RAK3172's 2.0 V minimum.
TI's guaranteed 93 mA output point is for a different operating condition, so
the model explicitly stays blocked pending room/cold exact-assembly transients.

The connected RTL-SDR Blog V4 also passed enumeration and a 45-second ambient
433-435 MHz acquisition. The largest temporal excursion within 433.95-434.05
MHz was only 1.01 dB; a separate 5.26 dB transient appeared near 433.754 MHz.
`RTL_SDR_CTT_AMBIENT_SCAN_20260725.md` records the exact scan hash and limits.
This proves the external receiver fixture only—not a tag, decode, calibrated
sensitivity, or the onboard high-band RAK3172 path—so the CTT launch gate is
unchanged.
