and v14; v15 advances the evidence and guarded HIL tooling, not the flight
bytes. The immutable verifier passes with zero failures. The exact ELF SHA-256

  exact-v15 median, 0.042 uA apart, with every selected 5 s bin below 10 uA.
- Nearby LongFast stimulus remains available. The protocol-level passive run
  observed 50 live packets from 12 opaque sources, and the fresh 60 s SDR
  capture found three LongFast-duration candidates. A second StratoLink is
  still required to prove v15 forwarding, B2B, and CTT emulation over RF.

The remaining launch work is now physical or production-facing rather than a
missing single-board firmware implementation: clear-sky GNSS, fitted-store
and cold endurance, two-node RF, exact-assembly antenna sweep, and backend
migration, deployment, registration, and regional credential validation.

Prepared during the connected-board qualification on 2026-07-24/25. This
ledger answers a narrower question than the launch-readiness matrix: for every
Flight-3 lesson or newly introduced flight subsystem, what changed, what direct
evidence covers the current implementation, and what evidence is still absent?

`PROVEN` below applies only to the stated layer. A host test does not prove RF,
a compile does not prove energy behavior, and a precursor-image soak does not
prove the exact frozen flight binary.

## Baseline and scale

- Flight-3 ran from 2026-05-17 through 2026-05-29 and returned 38 deduplicated
  GPS fixes over roughly 9,400 km before its last contact near Málaga.
- Its strongest direct success was a US915-to-EU868 circumnavigation segment
  with a working regional rejoin.
- Its strongest failure signals were cached/stale coordinates, one impossible
  coordinate/pressure row, an eight-day expected Atlantic coverage gap, a
  later 24.5-hour land-coverage gap, and insufficient onboard observability to
  distinguish RF, power, reset, GPS, and scheduling causes.
- From the first post-flight firmware fix (`9f044ba`, 2026-06-02) through
  `HEAD` (`21dd229`, 2026-07-24), committed flight work added approximately
  15,319 lines across firmware and supporting qualification analysis.
- The present dirty tree adds the counter journals, cryptographic vectors,
  exact-ELF health manifest, sensor recovery, stricter GPS freshness, radio
  transaction recovery, region lease, 40-byte remote observability, retained
  command/relay coherence, and deployment paths described below.

## Evidence that does not transfer

The following distinctions are mandatory in the final verdict:

1. The running 16-hour soak image is a precursor with a bench-seeded US region
   and `RELAY_SOLAR_MIN_MV=0`. Its source continuity, TTN, link, and sensor
   evidence is valid. Its cadence is consistent with the configured CTT and
   long shared-radio opportunity, but actual entry/traffic/restore and
   watchdog-housekeeping claims still require the post-soak counter snapshot.
   It spends little or no time in STOP1 and does not prove either sleep current
   or the corrected final binary.
2. Sanitized host suites prove pure logic and memory/undefined-behavior
   invariants only. They do not prove the STM32WL peripheral, RF, GNSS, power,
   or antenna paths.
3. The prior 18.3-hour relay study proves the shared-radio concept and real
   local Meshtastic traffic. It does not replace a final-image PHY-restoration
   check.
4. A single StratoLink board cannot prove two-node B2B over-air forwarding,
   ACK return, collision behavior, or authenticated command transport.
5. Exact CTT vectors cannot prove the 434 MHz front end or sensitivity. The
   fitted high-band RAK3172 is not RAK's 434 MHz module, so the StratoLink-2
   flight default now disables CTT. A compatible tag and launch-relevant link
   margin are required before re-enabling it.
6. PPK2 voltage-source continuity cannot qualify the flight energy store while
   the supercap is physically absent. The exact part is specified at 0.8-1.2 F;
   at its minimum capacitance, the baseline-only room screen is already under
   12 h when the measured 35 µA sleep current and specified 6 µA leakage limit
   are combined. Active cycles, cold, aging, and sag make fitted-cap darkness
   HIL mandatory.
7. Historical Flight-3 reception and NEC models cannot replace an S11/VSWR
   sweep of the exact soldered StratoLink-2 antenna/counterpoise assembly.
8. Local migration SQL and parsers cannot prove production wildlife/B2B
   storage while the production tables are absent.
9. Flight 3's 3.322 V minimum is not brownout metrology. Flown commit
   `c4bd109` converted the divider with fixed 3.3 V and explicitly ignored
   runtime VDDA; in buck dropout the ratiometric error produces a false
   ~3.32 V plateau while actual VSTOR continues falling. Current source
   refreshes VREFINT per VSTOR read and fails invalid references closed, but
   exact-image low-rail ADC/TX/reset HIL remains required. See
   `flight3_vstor_floor_audit.py`.
10. Component-limit arithmetic cannot qualify the present low-rail gates. The
    source-bound `low_rail_load_audit.py` shows that a pessimistic ohmic model
    leaves 3.301 V during the MAX-M10S 100 mA startup surge at the 3.600 V GPS
    floor and 2.868 V during modeled +14 dBm TX at the 3.000 V floor. Those
    clear the strapped GNSS 2.7 V V_IO minimum and RAK3172 2.0 V VCC minimum,
    but the GPS case is 11 mV below the 3.312 V buck setpoint and TI's 93 mA
    guaranteed output point uses a different 3.3-to-1.8 V condition. Exact
    room/cold VSTOR/VOUT/current/reset/TX transients remain mandatory.
