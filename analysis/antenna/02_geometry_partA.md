## GPS integrity gate — bigger than expected
Post-launch uplinks classified per region stream (`_gps.py`):
- US `stratolink-3`:    FRESH **32** / STALE 83 / NOGPS 24
- EU `stratolink-3-eu`: FRESH **7**  / STALE 133 / NOGPS 2
- **ALL: FRESH 39 / STALE 216 / NOGPS 26 → only 14% of post-launch uplinks carried a fresh position.**

The 39 fresh fixes match the flight notes' independent manual count ("38 fresh after dedupe").
The last ascent fresh fix before the freeze is `36.616°N −121.572°W 6924 m 32 sats` — exactly the
frozen tuple in `firmware/GPS_BUG_BOARD2.md`. Classifier validated.

The **EU/Spain leg was 95% stale** (7/142 fresh): we essentially do not know where the balloon
was for most of the European flight. (Pressure is still fresh during stale-GPS bursts, so altitude
is recoverable later via the barometer — useful for a future pass, not needed here.)

Geolocated receptions (gateway coords + balloon coords both present): **1226 total →
50 FRESH (used), 1176 STALE (discarded, 96%).** The stale share is so high here because the dense
EU gateway cluster heard the same frozen position hundreds of times.

## The design-driving numbers — split by flight phase (this is the real story)
The fresh sample is ascent-heavy (the clean SF climb). Geometry differs by altitude, so split:

**FLOAT ≥8 km (n=20, alt median 10,020 m — the OPERATIONAL condition):**
- depression median **6.0°**, **85% within 10° of horizon**, **max only 12.1°**
- gateway elevation median 5.5°, 85% ≤10°, max 11.4°
- slant range median 102 km, max 252 km

**ASCENT <8 km (n=30, alt median 4,046 m):**
- depression median 13.3°, 30% within 10°, **max 39.7°** (near horizon → higher angles at low alt)
- slant range median 47 km, max 92 km

**Azimuth:** concentration |R| = 0.27 (0 = uniform all-around, 1 = single direction) → receptions
come from nearly all bearings. Azimuthal omnidirectionality is a hard requirement.

## Takeaways for antenna design
1. **At float, 85% of links are within 10° of the horizon (max 12°).** The antenna's entire job is
   maximum gain at and just below the local horizon. Energy toward nadir is wasted — no gateway was
   ever near-overhead at float.
2. **Azimuthal omni is mandatory** (|R|=0.27; the balloon spins freely).
3. **Design for 0–~15° below horizon at float, with graceful gain out to ~40° for ascent/contingency.**
   A pattern that only spikes at exactly 0° would miss the ascent phase and any altitude excursion.
4. Caveat — small clean sample (50 receptions, 20 at float). The *qualitative* conclusion is
   geometrically necessary (A2 is pure physics, independent of data) and the float subset confirms it;
   the *precise* percentages carry n=20 uncertainty. Part B leans on the theoretical angle
   distribution and uses these points as validation.
